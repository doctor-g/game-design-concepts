## About this site

This site provides a web-accessible version of [Game Design Concepts by Ian Schreiber](https://gamedesignconcepts.wordpress.com/), which is an excellent free online resource for learning game design. Schreiber's work is reproduced here under the terms of the [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 United States License](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/us/).
The text has been lightly edited during transcription, removing references to the online forums that were part of his original online course.

Schreiber's syllabus included one required text and two recommended texts. 

Required:
- B. Brathwaite & I. Schreiber. _Challenges for Game Designers_. CreateSpace Independent Publishing Platform, 2008.

Recommended:
- S. McCloud. _Understanding Comics_. William Morrow Paperbacks, 1994.
- R. Koster. _A Theory of Fun for Game Design_.  Paraglyph Press, 2004.
